Delivery & Returns

Delivery

We ship worldwide from our West African suppliers to your door.

  • Free delivery on orders over $60.
  • Standard delivery costs $4.99 on orders under $60.
  • Estimated delivery time is 5–10 business days internationally, depending on your country and customs processing.

You'll get an email confirmation the moment your order is placed, and you can see your full order history any time from My Account.

Returns

You have 14 days from delivery to return an item that isn't right for you, unused and in its original packaging, for a full refund of the item price.

To start a return, email us at emmy4toz2010@gmail.com with your order number and we'll take it from there.
