From the Same Kitchen

Born from Faaji & Brew Palace

Faaji & Brew Palace opened its doors as a West African fine dining restaurant, built around the idea that a meal could carry culture as well as flavour. Almost from the first month, guests started asking where they could buy the fabrics hanging on the walls, the spices behind the day's specials, and the beads our staff wore on quieter evenings.

AfroMart grew out of answering that question properly. We now work directly with small makers, weavers, and traders across West Africa, buying at fair prices and shipping worldwide.

Every product on this site comes with a short note on where it is from and who made it, because that context is part of what you are paying for.
